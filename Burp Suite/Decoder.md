**Burp Decoder** is used to transform and inspect data by encoding, decoding, hashing, and converting it between common formats. It helps you quickly understand opaque values and prepare data for testing or replay in other Burp tools.

## **Encoding/Decoding**

**Encoding/decoding** is used to convert data between readable and encoded representations so you can understand how values are being transported, stored, or processed by the application.

## **Hashing**

**Hashing** is used to generate a one-way digest of data so you can identify how a value may be being transformed or produce the hashed form needed for testing.
