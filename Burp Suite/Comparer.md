
**Burp Comparer** is used to compare two pieces of data and highlight subtle differences between them. It helps you spot meaningful changes in requests or responses that may indicate different application behavior, valid input, or vulnerability-related effects.

## **Example**

For example, when performing a login bruteforce or credential stuffing attack with Intruder, you may wish to compare two responses with different lengths to see where the differences lie and whether the differences indicate a successful login.
